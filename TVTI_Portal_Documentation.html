1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives

The Twintec Vocational Training Institute (TVTI) platform is a unified cross-platform management ecosystem. It provides dedicated role-based portals for Students, Instructors, and Administrators. Key recent milestones include complete hardening against screen capture and recording, unification of modular video lectures and PDF study materials, intelligent practical slot scheduling with multi-tier conflict resolution, automated real-time notifications, and glassmorphic responsive authentication screens.

4. Advanced Screen Recording & Content Protection

To preserve proprietary vocational training curriculum, a multi-layer anti-piracy system was developed:

Hardware & Operating System Level Security Hardened
  • Android (FLAG_SECURE): Configured native window flags that force black screens when Android OS screen recorder, external capture cards, or third-party apps attempt to capture the viewport.
  • iOS (ExpoScreenCapture): Native iOS recording detection that immediately hides the media layer and replaces video playback with a security blackout shield.
  • AppState Transition Guard: Video player auto-pauses and dims when mobile applications are minimized or switched to split-screen/pip mode.
Desktop & Web Capture Defense Security Hardened
  • Screen Capture API Blocking: Trapped navigator.mediaDevices.getDisplayMedia and getDisplayMediaWithStream to reject unauthorized web capture requests.
  • Shortcut Interception: Blocks PrintScreen, F12, Ctrl+Shift+I (Inspect Element), and Ctrl+Shift+S with automatic visual warnings.
  • Window Focus Polling: 400ms interval polling checks document.hasFocus(). If OS snipping tools or Game Bar overlays take focus, the video element is blanked out immediately.
  • Moving Anti-Piracy Watermark: Displays dynamic student identity data (Student Name, Registration/Index Number, Timestamp) floating randomly across the video feed every 4 seconds, rendering screen recordings forensically traceable.

6. Enhanced Entity-Relationship (EER) Architecture

The TVTI Portal relies on a structured relational data model on MongoDB. Below is the Entity-Relationship matrix mapping primary entities, relations, and cardinalities:

Parent Entity Relationship Child Entity Cardinality Description
USER Specialization STUDENT / INSTRUCTOR / ADMIN 1 : 0..1 Disjoint role specialization based on role enum.
COURSE Offers BATCH 1 : M One course schedules multiple batches over time.
BATCH Enrolls ENROLLMENT 1 : M A batch holds multiple student enrollments.
STUDENT Enrolls in ENROLLMENT 1 : M Students can enroll in multiple training batches.
BATCH Assigns INSTRUCTOR M : N Multiple instructors assigned via instructor_ids[].
BATCH Hosts PRACTICE_SLOT 1 : M Batches schedule weekly practical lab sessions.
INSTRUCTOR Supervises PRACTICE_SLOT 1 : M Strictly limited to 1 practical slot per day.
PRACTICE_SLOT Reservations SLOT_BOOKING 1 : M Students reserve lab seats up to slot capacity.
STUDENT Books SLOT_BOOKING 1 : M Unique compound index prevents duplicate bookings.
BATCH Content VIDEO (Lecture/PDF) 1 : M Unified video lectures and PDF study documents.
STUDENT Attendance ATTENDANCE 1 : M Logs presence, absence, late, or excused status.
STUDENT Evaluations RESULT 1 : M Records marks and grades for course assessments.
COURSE Applications APPLICATION 1 : M Prospective student admissions and payment review.
